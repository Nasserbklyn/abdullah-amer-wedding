# Certified Singles Replace Prelox and Performer 8

**Bottom line:** No single bottle on the market replaces both Life Extension Prelox and Performer 8 at the doses their evidence requires while also carrying a drug-screening certification, so the strongest replacement is **two separate single-ingredient products, each matched by lot number in the certifier's own database and started one at a time**. For Prelox's vascular role the top picks are **NOW Sports L-Arginine 1,000 mg tablets** (Informed Sport, newest batch tested 9 October 2026), **Kaged Citrulline** (Informed Sport, batches to 24 June 2026, listed by Sporter in Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, Qatar and Bahrain) and **NOW Sports Arginine & Citrulline powder** (named on Informed Sport's November 2026 certified list), plus 80–100 mg of genuine but uncertified Pycnogenol to rebuild the trial regimen of **80 mg Pycnogenol with 2.8–3 g L-arginine aspartate a day** ([Informed Sport: NOW L-Arginine](https://sport.wetestyoutrust.com/supplement-search/now-foods/l-arginine-1000mg-tabs); [Informed Sport: Kaged](https://sport.wetestyoutrust.com/supplement-search/kaged/citrulline); [Informed Sport November 2026 list](https://sport.wetestyoutrust.com/sites/default/files/generated-pdf/file.pdf); [Tian 2023](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10614297/)); in the UK and EU, **Pharma Nord Prelox at four tablets a day** reproduces that regimen to the milligram, but it is uncertified and four tablets runs past its own maintenance label ([Pharma Nord UK](https://www.pharmanord.co.uk/all-products/mens-health/prelox)). For Performer 8's drive role, **MST Testofen 600 mg** ranks first because its lots are BSCG Certified Drug Free on a test menu that names sildenafil-type drugs and because fenugreek carries the lowest liver-injury rating of the drive herbs, while **Core Med Science KSM-66 600 mg** (NSF Certified for Sport, lot 119510125) is the caffeine-free version of Performer 8's main ingredient ([BSCG: MST](https://www.bscg.org/brands/millennium-sport-technologies); [BSCG test menu](https://www.bscg.org/bscg-banned-substance-testing-menu-v070725); [LiverTox: fenugreek](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK548826/); [NSF listing 1778870](https://www.nsfsport.com/certified-products/listing-detail.php?id=1778870)). The realistic ceiling is modest: arginine adds about **2.74 points** on the 30-point IIEF erectile-function scale, mostly in mild ED, the drive herbs raise desire scores in men without ED, and generic daily tadalafil 5 mg, at **+5.4 points over placebo**, remains the benchmark every product here must be judged against ([EAU Guidelines](https://uroweb.org/guidelines/sexual-and-reproductive-health/chapter/management-of-erectile-dysfunction); [Porst 2014](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0302283813010245)). Because new erectile problems precede coronary symptoms by a mean of **38.8 months**, a man in his 40s should have his blood pressure, lipids, HbA1c and a morning testosterone checked before starting, and men on nitrates, within six months of a heart attack or stroke, on blood thinners, with liver or thyroid disease, with hormone-sensitive cancer or on glucose-lowering drugs need a doctor's clearance or a different product ([Montorsi 2003](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12932937/); [AUA 2018 guideline](https://www.auanet.org/documents/guidelines/ed%20website%20final.pdf)). One correction to this project's earlier reports shapes every ranking below: Informed Sport and NSF test for substances banned in sport, PDE5 inhibitors are not on that list, and neither programme publishes whether it screens for them, so only BSCG lots are confirmed to be checked for hidden sildenafil or tadalafil ([WADA Prohibited List](https://www.wada-ama.org/en/prohibited-list); [Informed Sport vs Informed Choice](https://sport.wetestyoutrust.com/about/sport-vs-choice)).

## A certified amino acid plus 80 mg of genuine Pycnogenol rebuilds Prelox

Life Extension's Prelox earns replacement on three counts. Its label prints only a "720 mg proprietary blend" of arginine hydrochloride, aspartic acid and Pycnogenol per tablet, its directions halve the dose from four tablets to two after 14 days, and it is licensed for US customers only, so a buyer outside the US cannot order it from Life Extension and a buyer inside cannot check it against the trials ([Life Extension Prelox](https://www.lifeextension.com/vitamins-supplements/item01373/prelox-enhanced-sex)); NIH's label database even holds a 2025 Life Extension Prelox label that still lists icariin, which the brand's own page no longer shows ([NIH DSLD Prelox search](https://api.ods.od.nih.gov/dsld/v9/search-filter?q=prelox&size=30)). What deserves reproducing is the trial regimen: **80 mg Pycnogenol plus 2.8–3 g L-arginine aspartate a day, taken as four tablets in two doses, continuously**. In Ledda's 124-man trial it lifted the IIEF erectile-function domain from 15.2 to 25.2 at three months and 27.1 at six, against 19.1 and 19.0 on placebo, and two trials pooled to **+8.9 points** — a figure from Horphag-linked authors, with risk of bias rated low, unclear and high across the three RCTs, best read as a ceiling ([Ledda 2010](https://europepmc.org/article/MED/20184576); [Tian 2023](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10614297/)). Three grams of arginine aspartate carries about **1.7 g of free arginine** ([Stanislavov 2003](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12851125/)), and Horphag's later "Prelox R" — 80 mg Pycnogenol, 1.92 g arginine, 1.2 g citrulline and 40 mg oak-wood roburins a day — restored erectile function within a month in a 50-man double-blind crossover ([Stanislavov & Rohdewald 2015](https://europepmc.org/article/MED/25664962); [OA Text review](https://www.oatext.com/review-influence-of-combinations-of-pycnogenolr-and-l-arginine-aspartate-on-mens-virility-and-fertility.php)). This pass found **no certified Prelox-type combination and no Pycnogenol single with any drug-screening certification**; ConsumerLab does not even test pine-bark products, because peanut-skin extract can fool the assays ([ConsumerLab](https://www.consumerlab.com/answers/does-pycnogenol-work/pycnogenol/)). The workable rebuild is therefore a certified amino acid plus trademarked Pycnogenol — a sensible but untested do-it-yourself regimen, not a copy of any trial.

Within the certified amino acids, the two products whose Informed Sport pages were read live on 8 November 2026 now rank above the one seen only through a search-engine copy, reversing the original shortlist's order after the audit found that the third product's listing had never been opened directly. **NOW Sports L-Arginine 1,000 mg tablets rank first**: Informed Sport has certified them since 26 January 2023 and lists 15 batches, the newest (3480646) tested on 9 October 2026, the most recent testing of any product in this report ([Informed Sport](https://sport.wetestyoutrust.com/supplement-search/now-foods/l-arginine-1000mg-tabs)). Each tablet gives 1 g of arginine from 1,250 mg of the hydrochloride, so the label's one tablet twice daily (2 g) already exceeds the arginine in the Prelox trials, and three tablets (3 g) equal the dose given alongside sildenafil in El-Wakeel 2020 and sit just above the 2.5 g that improved IIEF scores in Gallo 2020 — one tablet past the label, so agree it with a clinician ([Professional Supplement Center](https://www.professionalsupplementcenter.com/products/l-arginine-1000-mg-double-strength-by-now-foods); [El-Wakeel 2020](https://europepmc.org/article/MED/31267684); [Gallo 2020](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7261690/)). Arginine is the ingredient with the most ED evidence: the only single nutraceutical with significant efficacy in a 2024 network meta-analysis of 15 placebo-controlled trials, worth **+2.74 IIEF erectile-function points** in the EAU's pooling and a weak guideline recommendation for mild ED, while 1.5 g a day for 17 days did nothing ([Barbonetti 2024](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39279185/); [EAU Guidelines](https://uroweb.org/guidelines/sexual-and-reproductive-health/chapter/management-of-erectile-dysfunction); [Klotz 1999](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10743698/)). The listing covers Europe and North America and pictures only the 60- and 180-tablet bottles, so a Nahdi or Soukare bottle in Saudi Arabia counts as certified only if its batch number appears on the page; a Nahdi UAE page for the 120-tablet bottle showed an expiry date that had already passed, and this pass found no current UAE listing for any of the top three vascular picks ([Nahdi Saudi Arabia](https://nahdionline.com/en-sa/now-sports-arginine-1000-mg-180-tablets/pdp/102949090); [Nahdi UAE](https://nahdionline.com/en-ae/now-foods-l-arginine-1000-mg-120-tablets/pdp/101506676)).

**Kaged Citrulline, the earlier report's best overall pick, ranks second** only because its newest tested batch is older and citrulline's ED evidence is thinner. Its live Informed Sport page shows certification since 22 August 2017, 13 batch rows with the newest (22561) tested on 24 June 2026, and availability on every inhabited continent; Sporter lists it in Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, Qatar and Bahrain, with stock varying ([Informed Sport](https://sport.wetestyoutrust.com/supplement-search/kaged/citrulline); [Sporter Kuwait](https://www.sporter.com/en-kw/kaged-muscle-citrulline/); [Sporter Saudi Arabia](https://www.sporter.com/en-sa/brands/supplements/kaged-muscle/)). A rounded scoop holds 2 g of fermented L-citrulline with no malate, so three-quarters of a scoop gives the **1.5 g** of the only citrulline ED study, in which half of 24 men with mild ED reached normal erection hardness after a month against 8.3% during a placebo month ([Kaged](https://www.kaged.com/products/citrulline); [Cormio 2011](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21195829/)). That study gave everyone placebo first and was single-blind, so the recommendation rests on certification and safety rather than proof of benefit; citrulline's real advantage is that it raises plasma arginine more than oral arginine does ([Schwedhelm 2008](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17662090/)).

**NOW Sports Arginine & Citrulline powder ranks third but is the most Prelox-like tub**: one level teaspoon (about 3 g) supplies 1.5 g arginine plus 1.5 g citrulline — Prelox-level arginine with the Cormio citrulline dose, close to the amino-acid half of Prelox R — and two teaspoons give 3 g of each ([Professional Supplement Center](https://professionalsupplementcenter.com/arginine-citrulline-powder-by-now-foods); [Nahdi](https://www.nahdionline.com/en-sa/now-sports-arginine-citrulline-powder-340-g/pdp/102819674)). Its batch record (3450953, tested 26 March 2026) comes from a search-engine copy of the Informed Sport page; Informed Sport's own November 2026 certified list names the product, but that list still carries a citrulline withdrawn in September (see the avoid list), so it corroborates rather than proves current status ([Informed Sport](https://sport.wetestyoutrust.com/supplement-search/now-foods/now-sports-arginine-citrulline); [Informed Sport November 2026 list](https://sport.wetestyoutrust.com/sites/default/files/generated-pdf/file.pdf)). One reseller prints a 10 g serving of 5 g plus 5 g, so dose by weight, and NOW's 500/250 mg capsules are a different, uncertified product ([myGP](https://store.mygp.com/products/arginine-and-citrulline-powder-now-foods-340-grams)). **NOW Sports L-Arginine Powder (fourth)** lets a man take Menafra 2022's 6 g a day — the dose that raised median IIEF-6 from 20 to 24 over three months in 98 men while placebo stayed flat — but its US label gives 6 g per two teaspoons and a Canadian label 4.6 g, and its indexed Informed Sport copy (newest batch 3461663, June 2026) predates October, so weigh the dose and match the batch ([Menafra 2022](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s40618-021-01704-3); [Nahdi](https://www.nahdionline.com/en-sa/now-sports-arginine-powder-1-kg/pdp/102949073); [Vitasave](https://www.vitasave.ca/l-arginine-powder.html); [Informed Sport](https://sport.wetestyoutrust.com/supplement-search/now-foods/now-sports-l-arginine-powder)). **Holland & Barrett's Precision Engineered L-Arginine 1000 mg (fifth)** is the easiest certified arginine in the UK and Ireland, with an indexed listing of 15 batches through 16 July 2026, but its directions and salt form were not captured ([Informed Sport](https://sport.wetestyoutrust.com/supplement-search/precision-engineered-holland-barrett/l-arginine-1000mg); [Holland & Barrett](https://www.hollandandbarrett.com/shop/product/larginine-1000mg-100-tablets-6100004716)). These four replace the uncertified Life Extension L-Arginine 700 mg capsules that this project's earlier report suggested as a top-up, because the earlier search had found no certified pure arginine ([Life Extension L-Arginine](https://www.lifeextension.com/vitamins-supplements/item01624/l-arginine-caps)).

The Pycnogenol half has to come from an uncertified bottle, so the test is genuine trademarked Pycnogenol at the trial dose. **Pharma Nord Bio-Pycnogenol 40 mg** makes 80 mg exact at two tablets a day in the UK and EU (skip its 160 mg first week); **Life Extension Pycnogenol 100 mg** (one capsule, with 4 mg vitamin C) is sold by Nahdi in the UAE and Saudi Arabia and suits a Life Extension customer who wants a disclosed dose; **Solgar Pycnogenol 100 mg** is stocked by Life Pharmacy in the UAE and Saudi Arabia and by Sporter in Saudi Arabia, but Solgar directs 200 mg a day, so one capsule is the limit ([Pharma Nord](https://www.pharmanord.co.uk/all-products/heart-health-circulation/pycnogenol-supplements); [NIH DSLD 182070](https://api.ods.od.nih.gov/dsld/s3/pdf/182070.pdf); [Nahdi UAE](https://www.nahdionline.com/en-ae/life-extension-pycnogenol-100-mg-60-veg-caps/pdp/103768821); [Life Pharmacy](https://lifepharmacy.com/product/solgar-pycnogenol-100-mg-vegetable-capsules-30-s); [Solgar](https://www.solgar.com/?p=411)). Life Extension ranks above Solgar because its only FDA action on record is a 2017 letter over online disease claims, whereas Solgar's record was not checked ([FDA warning letter](https://www.fda.gov/inspections-compliance-enforcement-and-criminal-investigations/warning-letters/life-extension-foundation-buyers-club-inc-500619-02012017)). Healthy Origins' Pycnogenol, the earlier report's do-it-yourself pick, still rests on a brand "third-party tested" claim with no named certifier, and no pine-bark product appears in NSF's Certified for Sport directory, so it drops out ([Healthy Origins](https://healthyorigins.com/products/pycnogenol-100mg-veggie); [NSF directory](https://www.nsfsport.com/certified-products/search-results.php?search=ashwagandha)). Alone, Pycnogenol has one 21-man ED trial that a systematic review called very preliminary; its case rests on the uncontrolled 2003 sequence in which normal erections rose from 5% of men on arginine alone to 80% once 80 mg Pycnogenol was added ([Borrelli 2018](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29633089/); [Stanislavov 2003](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12851125/)).

**Pharma Nord Prelox is the only like-for-like replacement** and the natural choice in the UK and the eight EU states where Horphag licenses it to Pharma Nord: the label itemises **1,400 mg L-arginine aspartate, 100 mg taurine and 40 mg Pycnogenol per two tablets**, so four tablets a day equal the trial dose to the milligram ([Pharma Nord UK](https://www.pharmanord.co.uk/all-products/mens-health/prelox); [prelox.com](https://www.prelox.com/shop)). The catch is the label: four tablets only for the first two weeks, then two — the dose closest to Aoki 2012, which did not beat placebo on the erectile domain — with an instruction not to exceed the recommended amount, and Germany and Austria sell it as a food for special medical purposes for use under medical supervision ([Aoki 2012](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21618639/); [Austrian pharmacy](https://www.onlineapo.at/en/Prelox-tablets/RA5982982)). Staying on four tablets therefore needs a doctor's agreement; the trial tablets also contained no taurine, the product carries no certification, and no Gulf retailer stocks it.

The remaining vascular options are clean but weaker, or certified with a drawback. Healthy Origins Performance L-Citrulline (sixth) holds a ConsumerLab approval for identity and purity, probably from a 2023 sample of an earlier label, and sells only in the US and EU ([ConsumerLab](https://www.consumerlab.com/healthy-origins/?type=review)). NOW Sports L-Citrulline 1,200 mg tablets (eleventh) are easy to buy at Sporter UAE and Nahdi, but the "Informed Sport certified" claim repeated online does not appear in Informed Sport's database ([Sporter UAE](https://www.sporter.com/en-ae/now-l-citrulline-1200-mg); [Informed Sport NOW brand page](https://sport.wetestyoutrust.com/supplement-search/brand/now-foods); [Healthline](https://www.healthline.com/nutrition/viagra-foods)). Etixx Arginine 1000 (thirteenth) gives 3 g arginine in three tablets but adds 8.4 mg iron — roughly a man's whole daily requirement on top of food — and its indexed Informed Sport copy shows no batch after October 2025 ([Etixx](https://www.etixxsports.com/en/products/arginine-1000); [NIH ODS iron](https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/Iron-Consumer/); [Informed Sport](https://sport.wetestyoutrust.com/supplement-search/etixx/arginine-1000)). BPN PUMP (fourteenth) is NSF Certified for Sport in all four flavours, but a full scoop is a training dose — 4.5 g citrulline plus about 0.6 g inorganic nitrate, roughly twice EFSA's acceptable daily intake for an 80 kg man, and 1 g agmatine — so only a third of a scoop fits an ED-style regimen ([NSF](https://www.nsfsport.com/certified-products/listing-detail.php?id=1802800); [HEB](https://www.heb.com/product-detail/bare-performance-nutrition-pump-blue-raspberry/16807549); [EFSA](https://www.efsa.europa.eu/en/press/news/170615)). NOW's 500/250 mg Arginine & Citrulline capsules need five or six a day to reach trial doses, and Jarrow's Arginine-Citrulline Sustain tops out at 1 g of each amino acid within its own directions ([Fullscript](https://fullscript.com/en-US/catalog/products/arginine-citrulline); [Jarrow](https://jarrow.com/products/arginine-citrulline-sustain-120-tablets)).

## Testofen and NSF-listed KSM-66 replace Performer 8's drive half without its caffeine

Performer 8's current label, taken as directed (three capsules), delivers about **562 mg KSM-66 ashwagandha, 375 mg arginine and 150 mg caffeine** in a product whose FAQ calls it "non-stimulant" and whose maker concedes that the arginine studies it cites used "approximately 12 times" its dose ([Performer 8](https://performer8.com/products/performer-8); [Performer 8 launch release](https://www.usatoday.com/press-release/story/43980/performer-8-reviews-wolfson-brands-announces-u-s-launch-of-current-11-ingredient-mens-wellness-formula/)). Its drive effect therefore rests on the ashwagandha, and a replacement needs a drive ingredient at a studied dose without the stimulant. Two qualify. **Standardized ashwagandha root extract at 600 mg a day** — the trial abstracts do not name the extract, though secondary sources identify it as KSM-66 — improved total DISF-M sexual-function scores by 9.8 points over placebo in 50 men with low desire, and in 76 healthy men aged 30–50 it improved desire, satisfying sexual events and IIEF scores while raising ejaculate volume 36%, sperm count 38% and motility 87% ([Chauhan 2022](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35873404/); [Khanna 2026](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41766918/)). **Testofen fenugreek extract at 600 mg a day** improved arousal and orgasm sub-scores in 60 men aged 25–52 and, in 120 men aged 43–70, improved ageing-male symptoms, morning erections and sexual frequency over 12 weeks while raising total and free testosterone ([Steels 2011](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21312304/); [Rao 2016](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26791805/)). Both trial sets are small, enrolled men without diagnosed ED and are at least partly industry-linked, so these are libido products, not erection treatments ([NutraIngredients-USA](https://www.nutraingredients-usa.com/Research/Fenugreek-extract-shows-male-libido-boost-Human-RCT); [Natural Health Research](https://naturalhealthresearch.org/ashwagandha-root-extract-ksm-66-and-male-sexual-health-evidence-from-an-8-week-randomized-controlled-trial/)). Tongkat ali, the third candidate, mainly raises testosterone in men whose levels are low and did not improve IIEF-5 scores in a two-trial meta-analysis ([Leisegang 2022](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9415500/); [Kotirum 2015](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26365449/)).

**MST Testofen 600 mg ranks first** because it is the only drive product whose certifier explicitly screens for the hidden drugs this category is known for. BSCG's own brand table lists Testofen as a Certified Drug Free finished product with six lots, the newest (260551) reported on 19 August 2026, and BSCG's test menu names tadalafil, vardenafil and dozens of sildenafil analogues ([BSCG: MST](https://www.bscg.org/brands/millennium-sport-technologies); [BSCG test menu](https://www.bscg.org/bscg-banned-substance-testing-menu-v070725)). One capsule holds 600 mg Testofen standardized to 50% Fenuside, taken with water or the evening meal for at least 8–12 weeks, which matches Rao 2016 exactly; Steels 2011 tested Testofen with a mineral formulation ([MST Testofen listing](https://www.amazon.com/Testofen-Fenugreek-Standardized-FenusideTM-Certified/dp/B08NZ39151); [Rao 2016](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26791805/)). Fenugreek also carries the lowest liver risk of the drive herbs, a LiverTox likelihood score of E, meaning an unlikely cause of liver injury ([LiverTox: fenugreek](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK548826/)). Three practical cautions follow: BSCG lists identical lot numbers for MST's women's product Libifem, so check the product name and lot together; the label tells men with high blood pressure, diabetes, heart, kidney or prostate disease, or a psychiatric condition including bipolar disorder, to see a doctor first; and in the Gulf it arrives only through cross-border resellers such as desertcart Kuwait, where lot matching matters most ([BSCG: MST](https://www.bscg.org/brands/millennium-sport-technologies); [MST Testofen listing](https://www.amazon.com/Testofen-Fenugreek-Standardized-FenusideTM-Certified/dp/B08NZ39151); [desertcart Kuwait](https://www.desertcart.com.kw/products/270124694-mst-testofen-supplement-twin-pack-2-bottles-of-30-count)).

**Core Med Science KSM-66 600 mg ranks second** and is the closest swap for Performer 8's main ingredient. NSF listing 1778870, read on 8 November 2026, certifies the 30-capsule bottle (one capsule a day) for lot 119510125 only, with an NSF-hosted label reading "KSM-66 Ashwagandha 600mg Extract Vegan (Withania Somnifera) (root)"; the brand's 60-count bottle counts only if NSF's second listing, 1780038, shows its lot ([NSF 1778870](https://www.nsfsport.com/certified-products/listing-detail.php?id=1778870); [NSF 1780038](https://www.nsfsport.com/certified-products/listing-detail.php?id=1780038); [Core Med Science](https://coremedscience.com/products/ashwagandha-60ct-capsules)). The NSF page settles a conflict with one registry that had filed the brand under NSF's contents-only standard rather than Certified for Sport ([Verified Supplement Data](https://verifiedsupplementdata.com/ashwagandha/third-party-tested/)). The trials split 600 mg into two 300 mg doses, so one capsule matches the daily total (assuming the trials' unnamed extract was KSM-66) but not the schedule, and Examine notes that one of Chauhan 2022's own figures contradicts its reported testosterone rise ([Examine](https://examine.com/summaries/study/9D6av1/)). Ashwagandha's liver signal — a likely cause of clinically apparent liver injury, usually 2–12 weeks after starting — and a modest pooled rise in T4 make it the riskier of the top two, and NSF lists "Sleep Aid" among this product's uses, so expect drowsiness ([LiverTox: ashwagandha](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK548536/); [Fornalik 2026](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41740946/); [NSF 1778870](https://www.nsfsport.com/certified-products/listing-detail.php?id=1778870)). No Gulf seller was found.

**Momentous Tongkat Ali (sixth)** is the only tongkat with current, worldwide, every-batch Informed Sport testing — 16 batches, the newest (0155F6) tested on 24 September 2026 — but it is 400 mg of whole-root powder, whereas the trials used standardized water extracts, mostly Physta at 100–600 mg, so its dose has no trial basis ([Informed Sport](https://sport.wetestyoutrust.com/supplement-search/momentous/tongkat-ali); [Momentous](https://www.livemomentous.com/products/tongkat); [Leisegang 2022](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9415500/)). Tongkat products have been found contaminated with lead, mercury or undeclared sildenafil analogues, and Informed Sport does not publish whether PDE5 drugs are on its panel, so even certified batches are screened for sport-banned drugs rather than proven free of them ([OPSS](https://www.opss.org/article/tongkat-ali-uses-and-safety-dietary-supplements); [Examine](https://examine.com/supplements/tongkat-ali/)). ## No bottle covers both roles at trial doses, so two singles beat every combination

A sweep of multi-ingredient men's formulas found **no single bottle that covers both roles at trial doses and carries a drug-screening certification**: the multi-ingredient products with any certification record — Thorne Advanced Testosterone Support (NSF) and Transparent Labs Vitality (an indexed Informed Choice listing) — contain no vascular ingredient, and every both-role bottle is uncertified ([NSF 1822465](https://www.nsfsport.com/certified-products/listing-detail.php?id=1822465); [Informed Choice: Vitality](https://choice.wetestyoutrust.com/supplement-search/transparent-labs/vitality)). The combinations fail for three structural reasons. Capsule space forces under-dosing: an analysis of 25 products sold in Italy found 80% matched a "no expected efficacy" profile, and arginine "was always included in a dose below the minimally effective amount" ([Petre 2023](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37686709/)). Labels drift or hide doses, as the NIH database's Prelox-family records show: Life Extension's 2025 label still lists icariin that its web page omits, and Purity Products' Prelox Men's Multi hides 1.44 g of actives in a "Prelox Performance Blend" ([NIH DSLD Prelox search](https://api.ods.od.nih.gov/dsld/v9/search-filter?q=prelox&size=30)). And every added ingredient adds interactions without adding evidence, because no trial has tested any of these finished combinations. Five of the formulas swept here ended on the avoid list below for exactly these reasons.

**GNC Men's ArginMax** is the best-disclosed both-role bottle and still falls short. Six caplets print **3 g arginine**, inside the trial range, alongside 100 mg Korean ginseng extract, 100 mg American ginseng, 50 mg ginkgo and 1,500 mcg of preformed vitamin A ([GNC](https://www.gnc.com/sexual-health/236811.html)). The ginseng is about a tenth of the 1.8–3 g of root used in the ED trials, the ginkgo is a fifth of a 240 mg dose that itself failed against placebo, and the vitamin A is half the adult upper limit of 3,000 mcg before any multivitamin is counted ([Jang 2008](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2561113/); [Wheatley 2004](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15378664/); [NIH ODS vitamin A](https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/VitaminA-HealthProfessional/)). The audit points to a small 1998 placebo-controlled pilot of the original ArginMax formula, which may not match today's label and was not re-read here ([PubMed: ArginMax](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/?term=ArginMax)). The product is uncertified, one listing shows a three-caplet serving instead of six, and a certified single arginine does its one evidence-based job with fewer interactions ([HelloPharmacist](https://hellopharmacist.com/drug-supplement-interactions/supplements/arginmax-by-gnc-men-s-13218)).

Two single ingredients carry evidence in both roles and are the only genuine one-bottle candidates. **Korean red ginseng** improved IIEF erectile-function scores by 3.52 points across nine RCTs (low certainty), just short of the 4-point bar, with self-reported ability to have intercourse 2.55 times as likely, and a 2025 meta-analysis also found gains in desire, orgasm, satisfaction and testosterone; the EAU gives it the same weak recommendation as arginine ([Lee 2021](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33871063/); [Ho 2025](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41696741/); [EAU Guidelines](https://uroweb.org/guidelines/sexual-and-reproductive-health/chapter/management-of-erectile-dysfunction)). **BulkSupplements Ginseng Root Extract** (1 g at ≥7% ginsenosides, 75 mg found) is the identity-tested option, though its ConsumerLab approval comes from a 2021 sample in a row whose product name is masked and whose heavy-metal result is not reported ([ConsumerLab](https://www.consumerlab.com/reviews/ginseng-supplements/ginseng/)). **CheongKwanJang Everytime** (one 3 g stick of Korean red ginseng extract a day) matches the trials' gram range from a large vertically integrated grower, but it is uncertified and its 11.6 mg ginsenoside figure should be checked on the box, because the same figure appears for KGC's "Royal" variant ([JungKwanJang](https://www.jungkwanjang.us/products/everytime-korean-red-ginseng-extract-liquid-stick-3000mg-jungkwanjang); [Shilla duty-free](https://m.shilladfs.com/estore/kr/en/p/3006750)). **Tribestan**, Sopharma's Tribulus tablet, is the exact product of the only large Tribulus RCT — 180 men with mild-to-moderate ED, 1,500 mg a day for 12 weeks, IIEF +2.70 over placebo with gains in desire and satisfaction — but the trial used the maker's own product, the regimen is six tablets a day, Tribulus has rare liver, kidney and muscle-injury reports, and no Gulf pharmacy stock was found ([Kamenov 2017](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28364864/); [Relias Media](https://reliasmedia.com/articles/141517-tribulus-an-rct-supporting-its-use-for-erectile-dysfunction); [Tribulus case report](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10849413/)).

Ginseng is still best run as a separate 8–12-week experiment after the two-product plan rather than stacked onto it: no trial combines it with Pycnogenol-arginine or ashwagandha, it adds a warfarin interaction and a glucose-lowering effect in the same men the other products already affect, and stacking makes it impossible to tell which product did what — the conclusion this project's earlier comparison also reached ([Yuan 2004](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15238367/); [MSKCC: Asian ginseng](https://www.mskcc.org/cancer-care/integrative-medicine/herbs/ginseng-asian)).

## Start the vascular product alone, score it at 12 weeks, then add the drive product

The first step is a doctor, not a bottle. ED is an early cardiovascular marker: in men with angiographic coronary disease it preceded cardiac symptoms in 67%, by a mean of 38.8 months, and the Princeton IV consensus tells clinicians to treat men with ED "as being at risk for cardiac events until proven otherwise" ([Montorsi 2003](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12932937/); [Princeton IV](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39115509/)). For a man in his 40s that means **blood pressure, a lipid profile, HbA1c or fasting glucose, and a morning total testosterone** — the AUA defines deficiency as below 300 ng/dL on two morning samples — plus a review of current medicines, since thiazide diuretics, beta-blockers and SSRIs commonly cause erectile problems that no supplement fixes ([AUA 2018 guideline](https://www.auanet.org/documents/guidelines/ed%20website%20final.pdf); [EAU Guidelines](https://uroweb.org/guidelines/sexual-and-reproductive-health/chapter/management-of-erectile-dysfunction); [MedlinePlus](https://medlineplus.gov/ency/article/004024.htm); [J Fam Pract](https://cdn.mdedge.com/files/s3fs-public/Document/September-2017/6211_JFP_ClinInquiry1.pdf)). Three findings end the supplement plan before it starts: nitrate use, a heart attack or stroke in the past six months, and a SHIM of 11 or less (moderate or severe ED), the last because 2.5 g of arginine moved severe ED by just 0.1 IIEF points against 4.9 for daily tadalafil ([Gallo 2020](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7261690/)). **Generic daily tadalafil 5 mg is the evidence benchmark**: +5.4 IIEF erectile-function points over placebo across 1,913 men, with 66% of mild, 48% of moderate and 29% of severe cases returning to the normal range, the lowest treatment-related adverse-event odds of any PDE5 inhibitor in a 2026 network meta-analysis, and a licence for prostate urinary symptoms as well — though it shares the supplements' absolute bar on nitrates and riociguat ([Porst 2014](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0302283813010245); [Salonia 2026](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42296271/); [Cialis prescribing information](https://pi.lilly.com/us/cialis-pi.pdf)).

Switching from the old stack needs two rules. Stop Performer 8 before starting any ashwagandha product — doubling ashwagandha doubles the liver and thyroid exposure, and stopping also removes 150–200 mg of daily caffeine — and stop Life Extension Prelox on the day the arginine, citrulline or Pycnogenol starts ([Performer 8](https://performer8.com/products/performer-8)). For a clean baseline, wait four weeks off both, since Horphag reports that the Prelox effect "largely faded" within a month of stopping; a man who prefers not to wait should record his score while still on the old products and judge the new plan against that number ([prelox.com research](https://www.prelox.com/mobile/research)).

Score the same way every time. The SHIM (IIEF-5) has five questions scored 5–25, banded 22–25 (no ED), 17–21 (mild), 12–16 (mild to moderate), 8–11 (moderate) and 5–7 (severe); grade hardness on the four-point Erection Hardness Score as well ([Rosen 1999](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10637462/); [Kaiser Permanente SHIM form](https://mydoctor.kaiserpermanente.org/ncal/Images/Sexual%20Inventory%20for%20Men_tcm75-217040.pdf); [Mulhall 2007](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17888069/)). The bar for success is a minimal clinically important change, not any change: Cochrane's ginseng review used **5 points on the IIEF-5**, and on the longer IIEF erectile-function domain the threshold is 4 points overall, 2 in mild ED, 5 in moderate and 7 in severe ([Lee 2021](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33871063/); [Rosen 2011](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21855209/)). Because the SHIM tops out at 25, a man starting in the mild band (17–21) may be unable to gain 5 points; for him, a move into the 22–25 no-ED band is a reasonable stand-in, in line with the smaller 2-point threshold for mild ED on the longer scale (this report's inference). The bar matters because an unblinded self-trial flatters itself: placebo alone raised IIEF erectile-function scores by 1.78 points across 62 trial arms ([AUA 2018 guideline](https://www.auanet.org/documents/guidelines/ed%20website%20final.pdf)). The SHIM does not measure desire, so during the drive phase also keep a weekly note of desire and sexual frequency, the outcomes the KSM-66 and Testofen trials tracked with their own questionnaires ([Chauhan 2022](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35873404/); [Rao 2016](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26791805/)).

Run the vascular product alone for 12 weeks at its trial dose: three NOW arginine tablets split through the day (one past the label, so with a clinician's agreement), three-quarters of a Kaged scoop, one level teaspoon of NOW Arginine & Citrulline, or four Pharma Nord Prelox tablets in two doses with food ([Professional Supplement Center](https://www.professionalsupplementcenter.com/products/l-arginine-1000-mg-double-strength-by-now-foods); [Kaged](https://www.kaged.com/products/citrulline); [Pharma Nord UK](https://www.pharmanord.co.uk/all-products/mens-health/prelox)). When rebuilding Prelox from parts, start the amino acid first and add 80–100 mg Pycnogenol after two uneventful weeks, so that any headache, dizziness or bruising can be traced; the scoring dates stay at weeks 8 and 12, which still gives the full combination ten weeks. Re-score at weeks 8 and 12; Ledda's scores kept rising from three to six months, so a rising trend at 12 weeks can justify continuing, but a flat line cannot ([Ledda 2010](https://europepmc.org/article/MED/20184576)).

Add the drive product only after the vascular verdict, with the vascular dose unchanged: one Testofen capsule with the evening meal, or one 600 mg KSM-66 capsule a day, for 8–12 weeks ([MST Testofen listing](https://www.amazon.com/Testofen-Fenugreek-Standardized-FenusideTM-Certified/dp/B08NZ39151); [NSF 1778870](https://www.nsfsport.com/certified-products/listing-detail.php?id=1778870)). Sequencing it second keeps any liver or thyroid reaction attributable, because ashwagandha liver injury typically appears between weeks 2 and 12; the audit advises liver tests at baseline and at about weeks 6–8 for men on statins or other drugs that affect the liver, and NCCIH notes that ashwagandha's safety beyond three months is not established, so courses should end and be reviewed at that point ([Björnsson 2020](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31991029/); [NCCIH: ashwagandha](https://www.nccih.nih.gov/health/ashwagandha)). If 12 weeks of the vascular product and 8–12 weeks of the drive product have not cleared the bar, the next step is the doctor and the tadalafil benchmark, not a third bottle; arginine at 2.5–3 g alongside daily tadalafil was tolerated in trials, but only a prescriber should set that up ([Gallo 2020](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7261690/); [Xu 2021](https://europepmc.org/article/MED/33587304)).

## Safety: seventeen conditions restrict these products, and only a lot number proves testing

### Each ingredient brings its own risks and interactions

**Arginine and citrulline** (every vascular pick except the Pycnogenol add-backs, plus ArginMax) lower blood pressure, so they add to nitrates — a dangerous pairing — and to PDE5 drugs, antihypertensives and alpha-blockers such as tamsulosin; arginine can also raise potassium with potassium-sparing diuretics, add to bleeding with anticoagulants and lower blood sugar with diabetes drugs ([Mayo Clinic: L-arginine](https://www.mayoclinic.org/drugs-supplements-l-arginine/art-20364681); [Drugs.com: arginine and sildenafil](https://www.drugs.com/drug-interactions/arginine-with-sildenafil-232-0-2061-0.html?professional=1); [Drugs.com: arginine and tamsulosin](https://www.drugs.com/drug-interactions/l-arginine-with-tamsulosin-232-4042-2146-0.html?professional=1)). The one mortality signal is VINTAGE MI: 9 g a day after a first heart attack was followed by 6 deaths among 70 men (8.6%) against none on placebo, so arginine is not recommended after a recent heart attack, and the caution reasonably extends to citrulline, which raises plasma arginine more than arginine does ([VINTAGE MI](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16391217/); [Schwedhelm 2008](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17662090/)). Everyday effects are stomach upset, bloating and diarrhoea — rare at single doses of 3–6 g and common above 9 g, so split the dose — plus headache, gout and worse asthma; NIH notes that the safety of high-dose arginine beyond three months is unknown and that citrulline's months-long safety has not been adequately assessed ([Grimble 2007](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17513449/); [NIH ODS](https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/ExerciseAndAthleticPerformance-HealthProfessional/)). NOW's Canadian arginine label adds cautions for glaucoma and recurrent herpes, Natural Medicines advises stopping citrulline two weeks before surgery, and the audit added an inference worth acting on: ACE inhibitors and ARBs, the commonest blood-pressure drugs in men in their 40s, also raise potassium, so potassium should be checked after starting multi-gram arginine, especially with reduced kidney function ([Vitasave label](https://vitasave.ca/products/now-l-arginine-1000-mg-120-tablets); [Natural Medicines](https://www.vital.ly/trc/L-citrulline/monograph=794); [Drugs.com: arginine disease interactions](https://www.drugs.com/drug-interactions/arginine,l-arginine.html)). Etixx's iron calls for avoiding it with haemochromatosis and taking it apart from levothyroxine, and PUMP adds nitrate, agmatine with little long-term human data, potassium chloride and milk ([NIH ODS iron](https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/Iron-Consumer/); [Examine: agmatine](https://examine.com/supplements/agmatine/); [HEB](https://www.heb.com/product-detail/bare-performance-nutrition-pump-blue-raspberry/16807549)).

**Pycnogenol and Pharma Nord Prelox** inhibit platelets — 100 mg matched 500 mg aspirin against smoking-induced platelet clumping — which is a bleeding risk with warfarin, newer anticoagulants, clopidogrel or aspirin even though 27 studies recorded no increase in bleeding; Pycnogenol may also lower fasting glucose and add to blood-pressure drugs ([Pütter 1999](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10498385/); [MSKCC: pine bark](https://www.mskcc.org/cancer-care/integrative-medicine/herbs/pine-bark-extract); [Examine: Pycnogenol](https://examine.com/supplements/pycnogenol/)). Monographs based on Natural Medicines add a theoretical caution for immunosuppressant users and autoimmune disease, the ABC monograph flags pine allergy, and because uncertified pine-bark products can be cut with peanut-skin extract, men with a peanut allergy should buy only trademarked Pycnogenol from authorised sellers (the peanut point is an inference) ([WebMD: Pycnogenol](https://www.webmd.com/vitamins/ai/ingredientmono-1019/pycnogenol); [ABC monograph](https://v9.pycnogenol.com/fileadmin/pdf/ABC_Pycnogenol_Monograph_2019_-_Consumer_Patient_Information_Sheet.pdf); [ConsumerLab](https://www.consumerlab.com/answers/does-pycnogenol-work/pycnogenol/)). Pharma Nord's German leaflet warns of facial flushing or headache at first and asks men with severe hypertension, heart problems or many medicines to see a doctor before starting; one unverified customer review on its UK page describes a five-hour erection that needed emergency care; its 200 mg of taurine sits far below EFSA's no-adverse-effect level; and no Prelox safety data extend beyond six months ([Pharma Nord leaflet](https://www.rathaus-apotheken.de/pimg/docs/00089537.pdf); [Pharma Nord UK](https://www.pharmanord.co.uk/all-products/mens-health/prelox); [EFSA: taurine](https://www.efsa.europa.eu/en/news/efsa-adopts-opinion-two-ingredients-commonly-used-some-energy-drinks); [Tian 2023](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10614297/)).

**Fenugreek** (Testofen, Pure Power) can cause diarrhoea and nausea, a harmful drop in blood sugar at large doses, and allergic reactions, including serious ones; a 2026 scoping review adds a maple-syrup odour, possible low potassium and a theoretical warfarin interaction from coumarin-like constituents, so check INR on warfarin ([NCCIH: fenugreek](https://www.nccih.nih.gov/health/fenugreek); [Scoping review 2026](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC12858439/)). It is not hormone-neutral — Testofen raised total and free testosterone in Rao 2016 — and it is unsafe in pregnancy, so it should never be shared with a partner who is or may be pregnant ([Rao 2016](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26791805/); [NCCIH: fenugreek](https://www.nccih.nih.gov/health/fenugreek)). As a legume it may cross-react in men with peanut or chickpea allergy, a point the audit raised from case literature but did not re-verify ([PubMed: fenugreek cross-reactivity](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/?term=fenugreek+peanut+cross-reactivity)).

**Ashwagandha** (Core Med, Klean, BulkSupplements, Momentous, Transparent Labs, Nutriburst, and inside Thorne, Vitality and Performer 8) is rated by LiverTox a likely cause of clinically apparent liver injury, typically cholestatic with jaundice and itching 2–12 weeks after starting and resolving over one to five months after stopping, with fatal cases described ([LiverTox: ashwagandha](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK548536/); [Björnsson 2020](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31991029/)). Pooled trials show a T4 rise of 0.61 µg/dL; NCCIH advises against use with thyroid or autoimmune disease or hormone-sensitive prostate cancer and before surgery, lists possible interactions with thyroid hormone, sedatives, anticonvulsants, blood-pressure drugs, diabetes drugs and immunosuppressants, and says short-term use up to three months may be safe while longer use is unstudied ([Fornalik 2026](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41740946/); [NCCIH: ashwagandha](https://www.nccih.nih.gov/health/ashwagandha)). The audit also flagged case reports of ashwagandha-linked thyrotoxicosis, not re-verified here ([PubMed: ashwagandha thyrotoxicosis](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/?term=ashwagandha+thyrotoxicosis)). Never combine two ashwagandha products, and never combine one with Performer 8, which already contains KSM-66 ([Performer 8 launch release](https://www.usatoday.com/press-release/story/43980/performer-8-reviews-wolfson-brands-announces-u-s-launch-of-current-11-ingredient-mens-wellness-formula/)).

**Tongkat ali** (Momentous, NutraBio, Double Wood, Puresport, Biogen, and inside Vitality) has isolated reports of clinically apparent liver injury, including a 47-year-old who developed jaundice shortly after starting it; EFSA could not establish the safety of a tongkat root extract, citing liver and kidney effects in a chronic toxicity study, and OPSS reports EFSA's concern about possible DNA damage in animals ([LiverTox: tongkat ali](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK609015/); [Kaliounji 2024](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38646387/); [EFSA 2021](https://efsa.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.2903/j.efsa.2021.6937); [OPSS](https://www.opss.org/article/tongkat-ali-uses-and-safety-dietary-supplements)). It may lower propranolol levels and is flagged for drugs cleared by CYP1A2 and CYP2A6, a tongkat package insert contraindicates prostate cancer and glucose-lowering drugs, its testosterone effect could fall under WADA's "similar biological effect" clause for drug-tested athletes, and after EFSA's opinion its sale in the UK and EU sits in a regulatory grey zone (an inference from the audit) ([Examine: tongkat ali](https://examine.com/supplements/tongkat-ali/); [Biogen insert](https://biogen.co.za/wp-content/uploads/412108-tongkat-ali-400mg-package-insert.pdf); [EFSA 2021](https://efsa.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.2903/j.efsa.2021.6937)).

**Thorne Advanced Testosterone Support and Transparent Labs Vitality** carry their ingredients' risks plus their own. Thorne's label reads "If pregnant, nursing, or trying to conceive, do not use this product," its luteolin rests on unpublished company data, shilajit as a raw material can carry heavy metals, and its 15 mg of zinc counts toward the 40 mg daily upper limit from all sources ([Fullscript](https://fullscript.com/catalog/products/advanced-testosterone-support); [Life Extension magazine](https://www.lifeextension.com/magazine/2024/6/restore-youthful-free-testosterone); [Shilajit review](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38393486/); [NIH ODS zinc](https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/Zinc-Consumer/)). Vitality pairs two liver-signal herbs, adds piperine to raise absorption, and some listings add DIM, quercetin and boron; Thorne and Vitality must never be combined, since both contain ashwagandha and shilajit ([The Feed](https://thefeed.com/products/transparent-labs-vitality-testosterone-support); [NSF 1822465](https://www.nsfsport.com/certified-products/listing-detail.php?id=1822465)).

**Ginseng** (BulkSupplements, CheongKwanJang, and inside ArginMax) most often causes insomnia, less often rash, liver damage or severe allergy; it may worsen autoimmune disease, interfere with clotting and lower blood sugar, and short-term use up to six months appears safe ([NCCIH: Asian ginseng](https://www.nccih.nih.gov/health/asian-ginseng)). American ginseng blunted warfarin in a randomised trial, Asian ginseng with MAOIs has caused manic-like symptoms, case reports link it to liver injury with imatinib and to raised raltegravir levels, it activated the estrogen receptor in breast-cancer cells in the laboratory, and long-term intakes of 3–15 g a day have been linked, on weak evidence, to a "ginseng abuse syndrome" of nervousness, high blood pressure and insomnia ([Yuan 2004](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15238367/); [MSKCC: Asian ginseng](https://www.mskcc.org/cancer-care/integrative-medicine/herbs/ginseng-asian); [Examine: Panax ginseng](https://examine.com/supplements/panax-ginseng/)).

**Tribulus** (Tribestan) has rare but severe case reports: a 46-year-old reached a bilirubin of 48 mg/dL and a creatinine of 7.1 after two months of daily use, and a 71-year-old on atorvastatin developed rhabdomyolysis two weeks after starting 500 mg a day, although LiverTox judges the liver link unconvincing at typical doses ([Tribulus case report](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10849413/); [Statin case report](https://amjcaserep.com/abstract/full/idArt/943492); [LiverTox: Tribulus](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK583201/)). Natural Medicines-based monographs list antidiabetes drugs and lithium as interactions, the audit flagged priapism and gynaecomastia case reports without re-verifying them, and marketplace resellers relabel the product, so buy Sopharma-labelled packs from a pharmacy ([WebMD: Tribulus](https://www.webmd.com/vitamins/ai/ingredientmono-62/tribulus); [PubMed: Tribulus priapism](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/?term=Tribulus+priapism)).

**Saffron** (Natural Factors affron) is well tolerated at about 30 mg a day, toxic only at gram doses of 5 g or more, and calls for caution with anticoagulants and antiplatelets ([CSIRO review](https://publish.csiro.au/hc/Fulltext/HC22067)).

### How to verify a certification by lot number

Certification belongs to a lot, not a brand, and it lapses quickly: between late September and mid-October 2026, three listings in this small pool were withdrawn from Informed Sport (one is ranked above for its single tested batch; two sit in the avoid list), while search-engine copies and even the certifier's own monthly list kept describing a withdrawn product as certified ([Informed Sport: Puresport](https://sport.wetestyoutrust.com/supplement-search/puresport/tongkat-ali); [Informed Sport November 2026 list](https://sport.wetestyoutrust.com/sites/default/files/generated-pdf/file.pdf)). The check takes a few minutes per bottle. First, find the batch or lot number printed near the expiry date. Second, open the certifier's own database — sport.wetestyoutrust.com for Informed Sport, choice.wetestyoutrust.com for Informed Choice, nsfsport.com or the NSF Certified for Sport app, and bscg.org's certified drug-free database — rather than trusting a logo, a retailer badge or a search snippet; BSCG warns that a certification mark on packaging "alone is insufficient evidence of compliance," and USADA tells athletes to scan the label to confirm "a certified product and lot number" ([BSCG comparison](https://www.bscg.org/blogs/single/independent-supplement-testing-what-it-is-and-who-does-it); [USADA](https://www.usada.org/substances/supplement-connect/reduce-risk-testing-positive-experiencing-adverse-health-effects/)). Third, read the status line, because a withdrawn product keeps its page and its place in search results: Puresport's tongkat page now opens with "This product is no longer certified," and a withdrawn ashwagandha still appears in Informed Sport's ashwagandha search ([Informed Sport: Puresport](https://sport.wetestyoutrust.com/supplement-search/puresport/tongkat-ali); [Informed Sport ashwagandha search](https://sport.wetestyoutrust.com/supplement-search?search=ashwagandha)).

Fourth, match the batch number and the expiry date in the batch table, and expect quirks: Kaged's table lists batch 21655 twice with different expiry dates, Puresport's page names a tested batch that is missing from its table, and NSF certifies Core Med's 30-count lot but not necessarily the 60-count ([Informed Sport: Kaged](https://sport.wetestyoutrust.com/supplement-search/kaged/citrulline); [Informed Sport: Puresport](https://sport.wetestyoutrust.com/supplement-search/puresport/tongkat-ali); [NSF 1778870](https://www.nsfsport.com/certified-products/listing-detail.php?id=1778870)). Fifth, confirm that the form, size, region and name match: Informed Sport certifies NOW's Arginine & Citrulline powder but not the capsules and lists NOW's arginine tablets for Europe and North America only, NSF lists UK versions of BPN products separately and has no UK listing for PUMP, and BSCG gives MST's Testofen and Libifem identical lot numbers ([Informed Sport: NOW L-Arginine](https://sport.wetestyoutrust.com/supplement-search/now-foods/l-arginine-1000mg-tabs); [NSF](https://www.nsfsport.com/certified-products/listing-detail.php?id=1802800); [BSCG: MST](https://www.bscg.org/brands/millennium-sport-technologies)). Sixth, know what the programme tests: Informed Sport tests every batch before release for more than 285 sport-banned substances, Informed Choice tests retail samples rather than every batch, NSF Certified for Sport tests for 290-plus banned substances at a lot frequency BSCG describes as variable, BSCG tests every lot for 450-plus substances including PDE5 inhibitors, and ConsumerLab and USP check identity, strength and contaminants only ([Informed Sport vs Informed Choice](https://sport.wetestyoutrust.com/about/sport-vs-choice); [NSF](https://www.nsfsport.com/our-mark.php); [BSCG comparison](https://www.bscg.org/blogs/single/independent-supplement-testing-what-it-is-and-who-does-it); [USP Verified Mark](https://www.usp.org/verification-services/verified-mark); [ConsumerLab methods](https://www.consumerlab.com/methods/l-citrulline-supplements/l-citrulline/)). If the lot is absent, treat the bottle as uncertified; in BSCG's words, "If a lot is not listed it has not been tested" ([BSCG: MST](https://www.bscg.org/brands/millennium-sport-technologies)). Where the bottle comes from matters as well: in one survey, 7% of sexual-enhancement products bought from pharmacies and health stores were adulterated, against 88% from gas stations, liquor stores and mini-marts ([Fischer 2023](https://www.dovepress.com/cross-sectional-survey-of-adulterated-sexual-enhancement-products-sold-peer-reviewed-fulltext-article-NDS)).

Seventh, check the regulator record yourself, because this search mostly could not. FDA's warning-letter and recall pages and the Health Canada, TGA and MHRA databases were blocked or out of reach for most products in this pass, so a product described here without a regulatory action has not been shown to have a clean record; apart from the FDA letters to Life Extension and Double Wood and the ExtenZe notice cited above, its record was simply not checked. Before ordering, search the brand and product name in FDA's Health Fraud Product Database and in its list of sexual-enhancement product notifications, where FDA posts hidden-drug findings ([FDA Health Fraud Product Database](https://www.fda.gov/consumers/health-fraud-scams/health-fraud-product-database); [FDA notifications](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/medication-health-fraud-notifications/sexual-enhancement-and-energy-product-notifications)).

## Conclusion

The replacement that emerges is less a product than a procedure. Neither Life Extension Prelox nor Performer 8 can be checked by the man taking it — one hides its doses in a blend, the other hides a stimulant behind a "non-stimulant" FAQ — whereas the replacements are commodity ingredients whose main virtue is that each bottle can be matched to a public batch list ([Life Extension Prelox](https://www.lifeextension.com/vitamins-supplements/item01373/prelox-enhanced-sex); [Performer 8](https://performer8.com/products/performer-8)). That moves the buyer's job from choosing a brand to verifying a lot, and the job recurs with every purchase: in the six weeks before this report, three listings in this small pool lost their Informed Sport status while search engines and the certifier's own monthly list still showed one of them as certified ([Informed Sport: Puresport](https://sport.wetestyoutrust.com/supplement-search/puresport/tongkat-ali); [Informed Sport November 2026 list](https://sport.wetestyoutrust.com/sites/default/files/generated-pdf/file.pdf)). Geography adds a twist for a Gulf buyer: the certified vascular products sit on Saudi, Kuwaiti, Qatari and Bahraini shelves, but the certified drive products are almost all US-only and reach the region through cross-border resellers, exactly the channel where a lot check matters most ([Sporter Kuwait](https://www.sporter.com/en-kw/kaged-muscle-citrulline/); [desertcart Kuwait](https://www.desertcart.com.kw/products/270124694-mst-testofen-supplement-twin-pack-2-bottles-of-30-count)).

The deeper lesson concerns the hazard this category is known for. Sport certifiers test for what athletes are banned from, and sildenafil is not on that list, so a sport seal on a herbal libido blend says less than buyers assume; only BSCG's lots are confirmed to be screened for PDE5 drugs ([WADA Prohibited List](https://www.wada-ama.org/en/prohibited-list); [BSCG test menu](https://www.bscg.org/bscg-banned-substance-testing-menu-v070725)). The safest design therefore keeps the hidden-drug risk low by construction — plain amino acids that nobody bothers to spike for the vascular half, a BSCG-screened Testofen for drive — rather than trusting any "tested" claim on a multi-herb formula. It also fixes the endpoint in advance: if the full procedure, done at trial doses, does not move the SHIM by 5 points in 12 weeks, the evidence points to a doctor and the tadalafil benchmark, not to another round of bottles. For a man planning children, the choice inside the drive half matters as well, since KSM-66 improved semen measures in its latest trial while Thorne's label forbids use during attempts to conceive ([Khanna 2026](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41766918/); [Fullscript](https://fullscript.com/catalog/products/advanced-testosterone-support)).
