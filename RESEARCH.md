## 4. Services Offered

### A. TLC / For-hire vehicle commercial auto (core niche)
Coverage for the four FHV classes, using correct TLC terminology (from the old site + NYC TLC rules):
- **Boro (green) taxis** — street-hail livery, hails allowed outside the Manhattan core/airports
- **Car service (livery)** — FHVs seating fewer than 6, affiliated with a livery base, pre-arranged dispatch, flat/time/mileage/zone rates
- **Black cars** — franchisees/co-op members of a black car base, pre-arranged, <10% cash business (Uber/Lyft are licensed as black car bases; "high-volume for-hire service" = 10,000+ trips/day; "e-hail" technically means app-hailing yellow/green taxis)
- **Luxury limousines** — pre-arranged, seating ≤20, <10% cash

Key regulatory facts for site copy ([nyc.gov/site/tlc/vehicles/vehicle-insurance.page](https://www.nyc.gov/site/tlc/vehicles/vehicle-insurance.page), [dmv.ny.gov for-hire requirements](https://dmv.ny.gov/insurance/for-hire-insurance-requirements)):
- Livery/black car, 1–7 passengers: **$100,000/$300,000 BI, $10,000 PD**, UM, PIP. 8–15 pax: $1.5M/occurrence; luxury limo (1–7): $500k/$1M.
- **PIP: $100,000 per person effective March 1, 2026** (down from $200,000) — resolved from primary sources: Local Law 90 of 2025 caps TLC's requirement at 200% of the state $50,000 minimum; TLC's adopted rule (Sept 17, 2025, amending 35 RCNY §§58-13, 59A-12, 82-14) and the live Chapter 59A rulebook both show $100,000. Sources claiming $50,000 confuse the state baseline with the adopted rule. Crashes before 3/1/2026 remain under $200,000.
- **FH-1 certificate** is the proof of coverage (carried in vehicle, on file with TLC); a lapse triggers 30-day plate/registration revocation. Personal-auto policies with rideshare endorsements do **not** satisfy TLC rules.
- **Carrier: American Transit Insurance Company (ATIC)** — sells only through "approved brokers" ([broker search](https://www.american-transit.com/broker-search.html)); programs include CAP Livery and Car Service ([caplivery.html](https://www.american-transit.com/caplivery.html), [carservice.html](https://www.american-transit.com/carservice.html)). **Blackridge's appointment with ATIC could not be independently verified** (dynamic, unindexed directory) — confirm with the owner. Alternative TLC carriers brokers use: **Hereford Insurance Company** (LIC) and **Maya Assurance** (LIC). No carrier named "Falcon" exists — never use that name.
- Useful process content: TLC driver license steps (chauffeur-class DMV license, 24-hr course + exam, drug test, WAV training, defensive driving, fingerprints); TC/"diamond" plate steps (base letter → FH-1/binder → [nyc.gov/lars](https://www.nyc.gov/site/tlc/vehicles/get-a-for-hire-vehicle-license.page) → DMV plate letter → register within 20 days → plate notification → inspection); FHV license pause means new vehicle licenses are effectively WAV-only. Documents for a quote: driver license, TLC license, registration/title/bill of sale, proof of address, lease docs, loss runs.

## 5. Market Context (ATIC, 2024–2026)

ATIC — historically insurer of ~60% of NYC's 114,000+ TLC vehicles — has been in a public solvency crisis since Aug–Sep 2024: NY DFS reported it insolvent by ~$665M ([Insurance Journal, Sep 2024](https://www.insurancejournal.com/news/east/2024/09/04/791291.htm)), widening to a filed negative $794.9M surplus at end-2025 (actuary estimate >$1B; [AutoMarketplace](https://automarketplace.substack.com/p/american-transits-financial-hole)); Marblegate and Inshur circled its assets but no sale, rehabilitation, or liquidation had completed as of Aug 2026, and ATIC continues writing and renewing TLC policies under DFS oversight, with 2025 rate increases of 2–8% (market-wide TLC increases 10–30%+). **Implication for site copy:** never claim ATIC (or any carrier's) financial strength, stability, or "best rates" — that is both factually hazardous and a violation of NY Ins. Law §§2122(a)(2)/1313 — and position Blackridge as a multi-market TLC broker rather than an ATIC-dependent one.
