# Discovery and challenge notes: Prelox and Performer 8 replacements

**Scope:** How the candidate pool for `reports/Prelox and Performer 8 replacements.md` was built, what the four discovery searches concluded, which product the challenge agents added, and what the run could not check.

## Takeaway

- Four independent searches (vascular, drive, combined formulas, and a sweep of the NSF, Informed Sport, BSCG and USP databases) returned 54 raw candidates, 53 after de-duplication.
- Nine verification batches checked all 53; the challenge agents proposed one more (Biogen Tongkat Ali), which was verified in a single pass. Final verifier verdicts: 9 recommend, 35 acceptable, 10 avoid.
- Some products were verified twice under slightly different names; the product notes keep both records.
- The ranking rule was safety first, then a certification confirmed in the certifier's own database, then full label disclosure, then dose against human trials, then a reputable maker with availability in the US, UK/EU and the Gulf.

## Gaps

- FDA's warning-letter, recall and tainted-product pages and the Health Canada, TGA and MHRA databases were blocked or unreachable for most products, so a missing regulatory action means "not checked", not "clean".
- Several certifier pages were seen only through search-engine copies; the report marks these as indirect, and the live page must be checked against the bottle's lot number.
- Informed Sport and NSF Certified for Sport do not publish whether their panels cover PDE5 drugs (sildenafil, tadalafil and analogues); BSCG's published menu does.
- No prices or costs were collected, by design.

KEY POINTS FOR THE NEXT STAGE
1. No certified Prelox-type combination exists. To rebuild Prelox with every amount known and a certified amino-acid half, pair one of candidates 1-5 with 80-100 mg genuine Pycnogenol (candidates 8-10):
- Prelox RCT dose (Ledda 2010; Stanislavov 2008): 80 mg Pycnogenol + 2.8-3 g L-arginine aspartate a day, as 4 tablets in 2 doses, taken continuously.
- Pharma Nord Prelox at 4 tablets/day reproduces this exactly.
- Prelox R-type option (Stanislavov & Rohdewald 2015; doses from Rohdewald & Stanislavov 2018 MRA): 80 mg Pycnogenol + 1.92 g arginine + 1.2 g citrulline (+ 40 mg roburins) a day. One level teaspoon of NOW Sports Arginine & Citrulline (1.5 g + 1.5 g) plus 80-100 mg Pycnogenol approximates it.
- Arginine alone: 2.5-6 g/day. Citrulline alone: 1.5 g/day.
- The salt conversions (e.g. 2.8 g arginine aspartate ≈ 1.59 g arginine; 1,520 mg arginine HCl ≈ 1.26 g arginine) are my own calculations.
2. Informed Sport and NSF screen for sport-banned substances. PDE5 inhibitors are not WADA-prohibited, and their inclusion in those panels was not confirmed; BSCG's panel explicitly includes them. For plain amino acids the hidden-drug risk is low anyway. Certification is batch-specific: match the bottle's batch number in the database.

SAFETY CONTEXT FOR ALL CANDIDATES (sources in project notes):
- ED appeared before cardiac symptoms by a mean of 38.8 months in men with coronary disease (Montorsi 2003, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12932937/), so a cardiovascular and endocrine work-up should come before any supplement trial.
- Arginine and citrulline add to the blood-pressure lowering of nitrates, PDE5 inhibitors and antihypertensives (https://www.mayoclinic.org/drugs-supplements-l-arginine/art-20364681). Arginine is not advised after a recent heart attack (VINTAGE MI, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16391217/).
- Ashwagandha liver injury shows up 2-12 weeks after starting; stop for jaundice, dark urine or itching (https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31991029/).
- The zinc upper limit is 40 mg/day from all sources (https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/Zinc-Consumer/).
- Judge any product after 8-12 weeks at the studied dose, scoring the IIEF-5/SHIM before and after.
