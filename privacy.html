Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Cuse Clouds Cannabis, operated by On The Bus Inc. ("we", "us", "our"), collects, uses, and protects information when you visit cuseclouds.com or our New York State licensed dispensary at 900 E Fayette St, Syracuse, NY 13210.

Information we collect

  • Age verification: we confirm you are 21 or older before granting access to the site; we do not retain a copy of any ID.
  • Information you provide: your name, contact details, and order information when you contact us or place an order.
  • Automatically collected: basic technical and usage data (such as IP address, browser type, and pages viewed) needed to operate and secure the site.

How we use it

To verify eligibility, fulfill and support orders, respond to inquiries, meet our record-keeping obligations under New York State Office of Cannabis Management (OCM) rules, and keep the site secure. We do not use your cannabis purchase data to advertise to you off-site.

We do not sell your personal information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. Consistent with New York cannabis regulations (9 NYCRR § 123.10(d)(5)), we limit our collection and use of consumer data to what is reasonably necessary.

How we protect it & data breaches

We maintain reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect personal information, consistent with the New York SHIELD Act (General Business Law § 899-bb). If a breach of private information occurs, we will notify affected individuals as required by GBL § 899-aa.

Sharing

We share information only with service providers who help us operate the business (for example payment, delivery, and IT providers) under confidentiality obligations, or where required by law or by the OCM.

Your choices

You may request access to or correction of the personal information we hold about you, or ask questions about this policy, by contacting us at cs@cuseclouds.com or (315) 214-4017.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time; the effective date above reflects the latest revision.

This policy is provided for general information and should be reviewed by legal counsel before reliance.
